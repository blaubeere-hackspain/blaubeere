# Product

**Positioning:** An internal finance planning tool that helps each company's finance team anticipate cash shortfalls and find ways to reach financial or business goals within a chosen timeframe.

**Buyer/users:** CFO, treasury, and finance teams; access is restricted to their authorised companies.

## Problem and value

A €2m balance against €4m due next month leaves a €2m gap before other receipts or financing. Teams need to understand today's position, where it is heading, and what would need to change to reach a target.

**Value:** earlier funding decisions, less manual investigation, and explicit trade-offs between growth, spending, and liquidity.

## Core workflow

1. **Assess:** current and historical financial health, trajectory, causes, and evidence coverage. The health score provides context.
2. **Forecast:** project cash and supported business metrics from historical data plus explicit finance-team assumptions such as growth, churn, margins, and collection timing.
3. **Set a goal:** choose one metric, target, and deadline. Other outcomes become constraints. Example: grow revenue 20% in six months while keeping cash above €100k throughout.
4. **Explore plans:** choose adjustable drivers and their limits. Compare suggested combinations of changes, projected results, cash impact, and trade-offs. Show the remaining gap if no tested plan meets the goal.

Financial goals include cash buffer, runway where meaningful, and debt repayment; business goals include revenue, retention, and margin where inputs support them. Label missing inputs, assumptions, and historical reconstructions. Planning never rewrites observed health or guarantees a result.

## Embat connection and scope

[Embat already provides treasury forecasting and risk monitoring](https://www.embat.io/treasury-management/cashflow). We build on that information with an explainable health trajectory and an internal goal-planning workflow.

Start with challenge CSVs and manually entered assumptions. No external ratings, investor reports, sharing links, action assignment, payment execution, or cryptographic verification.

**Success:** a finance user can explain a cash gap and compare plans against a dated goal. Validate the challenge score on unseen companies; measure warning lead time and false alerts where labels permit.
