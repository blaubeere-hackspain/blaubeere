## Trajectory inside the current dashboard — v2

- **Company/month navigation:** September 2024–August 2026. Keep a readable monthly chart/table with keyboard-accessible dates and numerical details. Do not create separate product pages for improvement and deterioration.
- **Current state:** `deficit`, `non_deficit` or `insufficient_evidence`, with robust base/trim bounds. This is operating-flow level, not stock of cash.
- **Direction:** `improving`, `stable`, `deteriorating` or `insufficient_evidence`. Compare recent three months with preceding three continuous months. A deficit may be improving; a non-deficit may be deteriorating. Show insufficiency separately from an unfavourable direction.
- **Why:** recent/prior known operating receipts/gross and payments/gross, base/trim interval differences and arithmetic contributions (`delta receipts ratio - delta payments ratio`). Display units, denominator changes and classification uncertainty. No causal language, SHAP or attributed model points.
- **Timeline:** separate provisional first signal/watch, actual confirmed alert at the second same-sign close, and retrospective event onset/confirmation. Confirmation is never retrodated. If showing the one-month baseline, label it comparison-only rather than the candidate's first confirmed alert.

The frozen v1 model may supply only eligible April–August 2026 points; earlier/ineligible dates are unavailable. Never stretch it across 24 months or treat a fall in probability as the direction rule. It remains retrospective, uncalibrated and not an official 0–100 health score.
- **Coverage/provenance:** inspect the six-month input window, eligible months, bounds, source version and missing reasons without exposing another company's data. Observed drivers explain inputs, not why the model assigned a probability.
