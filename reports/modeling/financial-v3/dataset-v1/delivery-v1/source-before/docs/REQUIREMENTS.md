## Regression contracts

The trajectory fixture tests exercise the rules below; application tests separately exercise the real sealed export, historical cuts and authorisation. Observed backtest performance is documented in the [v2 report](../reports/modeling/trajectory-v2/assessment/report.md). Passing implementation checks is not a claim of useful predictive anticipation.

- A company can be in deficit and improving, or non-deficit and deteriorating. Zero rounded lower bound is non-deficit; uncertainty crossing zero stays insufficient.
- Direction boundary ±0.05 is not stable. Base/trim disagreement or overlapping uncertain intervals does not become a confident label. Six-month windows never skip missing months.
- Watch at month one and confirmation at month two must keep different dates. An opposite sign begins a new watch; a continuing run crossing a split boundary is not reset to create a convenient new alert.
- An alert emitted at May close can match onset in June/July; July onset is only confirmed at August close. Lead measures distance to onset, not confirmation.
- Multiple alerts cannot share an event; an unmatched repeat with full follow-up remains false. Unknown follow-up never becomes a false alarm or a negative outcome. Observed unmatched events remain misses.
- Changes after an as-of cutoff cannot change its trajectory inputs under the same retrospective contract. Probability before April 2026 is unavailable, not a historical backcast.
- If event/alert support is zero, ratios or lead summaries are null where undefined. Small support must not produce fabricated CIs or performance claims.

## Preserve existing cash-mode checks

- €2m cash, €4m due, no other flows/funding and zero buffer implies €2m funding need; the existing €100k demo buffer makes it €2.1m. Later receipts cannot erase an earlier shortfall.
- Missing/stale balances, receivables, debt, accessible funding and business assumptions are explicit, not zero. Final-only snapshots do not establish historical knowledge.
- A plan reaching its final target but breaching its cash floor fails that constraint; no qualifying tested plan yields an unmet-gap explanation, not a claim that every plan is impossible.
- Growth/churn relationships remain explicit and are not double-counted or treated as demonstrated causal effects. Plans preserve baseline, source records and observed trajectory. Unauthorised cross-company access fails.
